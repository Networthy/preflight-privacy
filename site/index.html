Preflight

Privacy Policy

Effective June 15, 2026

Overview

Preflight is a Chrome extension that reviews a region you select in an email, newsletter, landing page, or similar web surface. It checks the selected content for copy, link, visual, and accessibility issues and displays findings on the page.

Preflight accesses and transmits review content only when you explicitly start a review. It does not monitor your browsing in the background, sell personal data, or use personal data for advertising.

Information We Process

Content submitted for review

When you start a review, Preflight may process:

  • Text and subject content from the region you selected.
  • Cleaned HTML and structural information for the selected region.
  • Links, link text, image URLs, image alternative text, and related metadata.
  • A screenshot of the selected region, including text visible in images.
  • The page URL, page title, viewport details, and supported platform information.

Depending on the content you select, this information may include personal communications, personal information, or other sensitive material. Select only content that you are authorized to review.

Account and service information

Preflight may process:

  • Your account email address and workspace or account identifiers.
  • Device credentials used to authenticate the extension.
  • Credit balances, usage totals, request identifiers, and service status.

Technical information

The service records limited operational metadata needed for security, reliability, abuse prevention, support, and cost control. This may include request time, request size, check type, duration, result counts, device and workspace identifiers, error category, and network logs such as IP address. Operational logs are designed not to contain raw review text, HTML, screenshots, prompts, or full model responses.

How We Use Information

We use information only to:

  • Authenticate your account and extension installation.
  • Perform the review you requested and return findings.
  • Apply saved preferences and selected-region settings.
  • Enforce usage limits and protect the service from abuse.
  • Diagnose failures, provide support, and improve review quality and reliability.
  • Comply with legal obligations and protect users and the service.

Service Providers and Disclosure

Review content is sent over HTTPS to the managed Preflight service. Preflight may send the portions needed for analysis to contracted infrastructure and artificial-intelligence providers, including Google Gemini services. These providers process data to deliver the requested review and are subject to their applicable contractual terms and data handling policies.

We may also disclose information when required by law, to investigate security incidents or abuse, or as part of a merger, acquisition, or sale of assets. We do not sell user data or disclose it for personalized, retargeted, or interest-based advertising.

Browser Storage

The extension stores saved region selectors, preferences, disclosure acceptance, account metadata, and device credentials in Chrome's local extension storage. Review content, screenshots, and findings are not persistently stored by the extension. Disconnecting removes local account metadata and credentials but leaves saved region settings until you delete them or remove the extension.

Retention

Raw review content, cleaned HTML, screenshots, prompts, and model responses are processed for the requested review and are not retained by the Preflight service by default. Preflight retains account, authentication, usage-ledger, billing, security, and operational metadata for as long as reasonably necessary to operate and protect the service and meet legal obligations.

An exported debug bundle is created only when you explicitly request one. It is downloaded to your device and may contain the submitted content, screenshot, request metadata, and findings. You control and are responsible for that downloaded file.

Human Access

Preflight personnel do not read raw review content as part of normal service operation. Human access is limited to circumstances permitted by the Chrome Web Store User Data Policy, such as when you give consent for support involving specific content, when access is required for security or abuse investigation, or when required by law.

Security

Preflight uses HTTPS for service communications, limits production host access to the managed API, stores hashed device-token representations on the server, and applies authentication, payload limits, usage controls, and other safeguards. No system can guarantee absolute security.

Your Choices

  • You choose whether to start each review and which region to submit.
  • You can cancel before accepting the first-review disclosure.
  • You can disconnect the extension to revoke and remove local credentials.
  • You can delete saved views or uninstall the extension to remove locally stored settings.
  • You may contact us to request access, correction, or deletion of account information.

Chrome Web Store Limited Use

Preflight's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs and Chrome extension permissions adheres to the Chrome Web Store User Data Policy, including its Limited Use requirements. Data obtained through extension permissions is used only to provide or improve Preflight's user-facing review functionality.

Children

Preflight is intended for workplace and professional communications review and is not directed to children under 13.

Policy Changes

We may update this policy as Preflight changes. The effective date at the top of this page identifies the latest revision. Material changes will be communicated through the extension, the service, or another appropriate channel.
