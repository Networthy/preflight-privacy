Preflight

Privacy Policy

Effective November 18, 2026

Overview

Preflight reviews emails, newsletters, landing pages, and similar web content before you send or publish them. It has three parts:

  • The Chrome extension reviews a region you select on a page. It checks the content for copy, link, visual, accessibility, and brand-guideline issues and shows the findings on the page.
  • The web portal is where you sign in, connect browsers, manage your workspace's brand guidelines, and manage billing.
  • The Preflight service runs reviews and stores your account, workspace, and review history.

The extension reads page content only when you start a review. It does not monitor your browsing in the background. We do not sell personal data or use it for advertising.

Information We Process

Content you submit for review

When you start a review, the extension sends the Preflight service:

  • The text of the region you selected, with each element's role, layout position, and computed style, and the email subject when you review a webmail message.
  • Cleaned HTML and structural information for the selected region.
  • Links, link text, image URLs, image alternative text, and related metadata.
  • A screenshot of the selected region, including any text that appears in images.
  • The page URL and title, viewport details, and supported platform information.
  • Fingerprints (one-way hashes) of the content and page address, so that related reviews can be grouped.
  • Results of checks the extension ran locally, and your review settings.

Depending on what you select, this may include personal communications, personal information, or other sensitive material. Select only content you are authorized to review.

Brand guidelines

Workspace members can upload brand guidelines (PDF, DOCX, PNG, JPEG, or SVG files) in the portal and edit the rules and voice settings derived from them. We store the uploaded files, text extracted from them, page preview images, and the resulting rules, examples, and citations.

Account and workspace information

  • Your name, email address, workspace name, and workspace role.
  • The names of browsers you connect, when they were connected and last seen, and the credentials that authenticate them.
  • Your plan, subscription status, credit balance, and usage totals.

Billing information

Paid plans are billed by Stripe. When a workspace owner or admin upgrades, they enter payment details on pages hosted by Stripe. Preflight never receives or stores full card numbers. We send Stripe your email address, your workspace name, and a workspace identifier. Stripe may also collect a billing name, billing address, and tax ID to calculate taxes. We keep Stripe's customer and subscription identifiers, your plan, the subscription status and billing period, and records of refunds, disputes, and fraud warnings that Stripe reports to us.

Feedback and support

If you send feedback from the extension, we receive your description, your name and email address, and the related review identifier. Unless you uncheck the option, feedback also includes a debug bundle: the reviewed content, the screenshot, the findings, and request diagnostics.

Technical information

The service records limited operational data for security, reliability, abuse prevention, support, and cost control. This includes request times and sizes, check types, durations, result counts, model usage and cost, device and workspace identifiers, and error categories. Our hosting provider's network logs may include IP addresses. For sign-in links we store a keyed hash of the requesting IP address and browser, not the raw values. Operational logs are designed not to contain review text, HTML, screenshots, prompts, or model responses.

How We Use Information

We use information only to:

  • Authenticate you, your workspace, and the browsers you connect.
  • Perform the reviews you request, check your links, and return findings.
  • Turn uploaded brand guidelines into rules and apply them to your reviews.
  • Show your review history and the share links you create.
  • Manage plans, credits, subscriptions, and payments.
  • Enforce usage limits and protect the service from abuse and fraud.
  • Diagnose failures, provide support, and improve review quality and reliability.
  • Comply with legal obligations and protect users and the service.

Service Providers and Disclosure

Review content is sent over HTTPS to the Preflight service. We share information with the following service providers only as needed to run Preflight:

  • OpenAI analyzes review content, including text, HTML, screenshots, and your workspace's brand rules and logo images. It also turns uploaded brand guidelines into rules. You confirm an AI-processing notice before a guideline import starts.
  • Google Cloud Vision reads the text in images in your review content. We send it only the parts of the screenshot that show those images, never the image addresses or the rest of the review.
  • Stripe processes subscriptions, payments, and taxes.
  • Railway hosts the Preflight service, its database, file storage, and the isolated machines used to check links.
  • An email delivery provider sends sign-in links.
  • YouTube and Vimeo receive the addresses of their own video links in your content, so we can check that those videos exist.

These providers process data under their contractual terms and data handling policies. We use paid AI and image-reading services under business terms that do not allow the provider to train its models on the content we send. If we change these providers, we will update this policy. Uploaded guideline files are scanned for malware and converted to text by Preflight's own isolated service. They are not sent to an outside scanning or OCR provider.

Link checking. To verify links, Preflight requests each link in the reviewed content from its own servers, sometimes in a headless browser. Each review uses a fresh browser and machine without your cookies or sign-ins. The websites and link-tracking services behind those links receive the request, including any tracking parameters in the link.

We may also disclose information when required by law, to investigate security incidents or abuse, or as part of a merger, acquisition, or sale of assets. We do not sell user data or disclose it for personalized, retargeted, or interest-based advertising.

Shared Review Links

You can create a link to a past review. Anyone with the link can view it without signing in until it expires (after at most 7 days) or you revoke it. A shared review shows the email subject, the findings, and the quoted text and links they refer to. If you include the email preview, it also shows an image of the reviewed content, which may contain personal details. The extension warns you about this the first time. Shared pages are marked so that search engines do not index them.

Cookies and Browser Storage

The portal sets one cookie, a secure session cookie that keeps you signed in for up to 30 days. The portal and extension do not use analytics, advertising, or third-party tracking scripts.

The extension stores the following in Chrome's local extension storage:

  • Saved regions and preferences, including the accessibility-check setting.
  • Your acceptance of the review disclosure and the share-preview warning.
  • Account details: your email, workspace, plan, and credits.
  • The credentials for your connected browser.
  • Short-lived identifiers that let an interrupted review resume.

On the page you review, the extension remembers the width and collapsed state of its sidebar in that site's local storage. The optional copy-evaluation tool stores your ratings, comments, and a copy of the reviewed content and screenshot in the extension's own browser database. It does not upload them, and you can export them as a file.

Disconnecting the extension revokes and removes its credentials and account details. Saved regions, preferences, and copy-evaluation drafts remain until you delete them or remove the extension.

Retention

  • Review history. Each review's findings, quoted text, link and image details, content excerpts, and screenshot are kept for 30 days, then deleted. We may enable retention of the full submitted text to support re-checking past reviews. It is off by default and never kept longer than the review itself.
  • Model inputs. Prompts, cleaned HTML, and raw model responses are processed for the review and not stored.
  • Shared links and their preview images last up to 7 days and are deleted when they expire or you revoke them.
  • Feedback debug bundles are kept for up to 30 days. Written feedback is kept as long as needed to address it.
  • Brand guidelines are kept until a workspace member deletes them. After a purge, only minimal metadata remains, such as a file name and a digest.
  • Sign-in links expire after 15 minutes and work once.
  • Account, usage, billing, security, and audit records are kept while your account is active and afterward as needed to operate the service, resolve disputes, and meet legal, tax, and accounting obligations.

Debug bundles and evaluation files you download are saved to your device. They may contain the submitted content, screenshot, and findings. You control and are responsible for those files.

Human Access

Preflight personnel do not read review content as part of normal operation. We may view it when you send it to us with feedback or otherwise give consent for support, when needed to investigate security or abuse, or when required by law. These limits are consistent with the Chrome Web Store User Data Policy.

Security

Preflight uses HTTPS for all service communications. We store only hashes of extension credentials, portal sessions, sign-in links, and share-link secrets. Shared links keep their secret in a part of the address that browsers do not send to servers. Uploaded files are malware-scanned and parsed in an isolated service without access to your account data. Links are checked on disposable machines. We also apply authentication, payload limits, usage controls, and other safeguards. No system can guarantee absolute security.

Your Choices

  • You choose whether to start each review and which region to submit.
  • You can cancel before accepting the first-review disclosure.
  • You can leave the debug bundle out of feedback you send.
  • You can revoke shared links before they expire.
  • You can disconnect the extension, or revoke a connected browser from the portal.
  • You can delete uploaded brand guidelines.
  • You can manage or cancel a subscription and update payment details through Stripe's billing portal.
  • You can delete saved regions or uninstall the extension to remove local data.
  • You can contact us to request access to, correction of, or deletion of your account, workspace, or review data.

Chrome Web Store Limited Use

Preflight's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs and Chrome extension permissions adheres to the Chrome Web Store User Data Policy, including its Limited Use requirements. Data obtained through extension permissions is used only to provide or improve Preflight's user-facing review functionality.

Children

Preflight is intended for workplace and professional communications review and is not directed to children under 13.
